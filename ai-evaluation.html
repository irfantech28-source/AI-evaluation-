Demonstration samples

Examples of how I evaluate

Fictional examples created for this portfolio, not client data.

Sample 1 · Response ranking
PromptExplain in two sentences why the sky looks blue.
Response AThe sky is blue because it reflects the color of the ocean.
Response BSunlight is scattered by air molecules, and blue light scatters more than red because of its shorter wavelength. That is why the daytime sky looks blue.

Ranking: B > A. A contains a factual error. B is accurate, clear and follows the two-sentence limit.

Sample 2 · Search relevance
Querybest way to learn typing for beginners
Highly relevant

Free beginner typing lessons with daily practice drills.

Partly relevant

An article about the history of the typewriter (right topic, wrong intent).

Not relevant

A store page for gaming keyboards.

Rated on user intent, not just matching words.

Sample 3 · Urdu translation QA
Source (English)Your account has been verified.
Candidate (Urdu)

آپ کا اکاؤنٹ تصدیق ہو گیا ہے۔

Corrected (Urdu)

آپ کے اکاؤنٹ کی تصدیق ہو گئی ہے۔

Meaning: preserved. Fluency: needs edit, the phrasing sounds unnatural. Label: edit, then approve.

Sample 4 · Content classification
Spam

“Win a free phone, click this link now!!!”

Safe

“What time does the library open?”

Abusive

“You're an idiot, nobody wants you here.”

Each label comes with a short reason tied to the guideline, and borderline cases are flagged rather than guessed.

Sample 5 · Urdu response evaluation
Prompt (Urdu)

پانی کو صاف کرنے کے دو آسان طریقے بتائیں۔

Response A

پانی کو کم از کم ایک منٹ تک ابالیں، پھر ٹھنڈا ہونے دیں۔ اس کے علاوہ آپ واٹر فلٹر بھی استعمال کر سکتے ہیں۔

Response B

پانی کو صفائی کرنے کے لئے دو آسان طریقے ہیں: پانی میں چینی ملا دیں اور رات بھر رکھیں۔

Ranking: A > B. A gives two valid methods in natural Urdu. B is a stiff, word-for-word style sentence and its method does not purify water (accuracy and safety issue).

Sample 6 · QA bug report
TitleLogin button stays disabled after entering a valid password

Environment: Android app, fictional build 2.4
Steps:

  • Open the app and go to Login.
  • Enter a valid email address.
  • Enter a valid 8-character password.
  • Look at the Login button.

Expected: the button becomes active.
Actual: it stays grey until the keyboard is closed.
Severity: Medium. Users can log in but may think the form is broken.

My review checklistRead the guideline first · check facts · check instruction-following · check language and tone · give a clear label with a short reason · flag anything unclear instead of guessing.
